How it works

A straightforward workflow for organizers and attendees.

  1. Register

    Enter attendee details and issue a QR pass from the registration form.

  2. Share the pass

    Download the generated QR code and give it to the attendee.

  3. Scan at entry

    Verify the pass at the gate and follow attendance on the dashboard.
